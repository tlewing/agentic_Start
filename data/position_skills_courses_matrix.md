### Rationale Notes

- **Safety is Mandatory for all field roles** (Apprentice through Superintendent, Safety). The generic Apprentice JD states: "All apprentices must study and follow GSL's safety policies." MD 9.2 requires all field employees to report safety concerns and follow safety P&P.
- **Leadership is Mandatory from Leadman up.** JDs explicitly require leadership competencies starting at Leadman 1. The Leadman MD requires "ambassadors of goodwill" and positive attitude.
- **Lean Principles is Mandatory from Leadman/Foreman up.** The Foreman JD states "Implementation of lean construction principles." The COO JD states "Advocates for lean construction techniques." The Leadman training includes Lean Construction Principles.
- **Software is Mandatory for Leadman/Foreman and above.** Foreman JD requires Excel, Word, Outlook, Bluebeam. General Foreman adds PlanGrid. PM requires project management software.
- **Culture & Values is Mandatory for all field roles and executives.** The Board JD anchors it: "Govern in accordance with its culture including its objective, vision, mission, and core values." Every field JD includes Ethics, Diversity, Teamwork, Dependability.
- **Management is Mandatory for Superintendent, PM, GS, Safety Director, and Executive.** These roles have explicit management/administration duties.
- **Procurement is Recommended for PM and Estimating.** MD 9.16 (Purchasing) ties directly to PM buyout responsibilities. MD 9.7 (Estimating) references subcontractor/supplier management.

### Rationale Notes

- **New Hire Safety Orientation is Mandatory for every field employee.** JD for Apprentice 1 lists "Jobsite Safety Level 1" and "Personal Safety Level 1." MD 9.2 requires safety orientation signature. Safety Coordinator Series (course 3.0) is specifically about Employee Safety Orientation.
- **Lockout/Tagout is Mandatory for all field roles through Superintendent.** Every Apprentice JD lists "Lockout/Tagout" as a required credential.
- **Safety Coordinator Series is Mandatory from Leadman 2 (who must complete GSL Safety Coordinator training) through Superintendent.** Foreman JD explicitly states: "Completion of all requirements to achieve certification as a GSL Safety Coordinator."

- **Critical Leadership Training is Mandatory from Foreman up.** Covers Extreme Ownership, leadership laws, and decision-making -- competencies explicitly listed in Foreman/GF JDs.

- **Performance Management courses are Mandatory for any role that conducts performance reviews** -- Foreman, Superintendent, PM, General Superintendent. The Foreman JD includes "Performance Coaching" as a competency. HR Manager oversees the system but doesn't directly deliver field reviews.

### Rule 6: Mandatory vs Recommended Distinction

**Mandatory (M):** The employee MUST complete these courses. The JD or MD explicitly requires the competency, the role cannot be performed safely/effectively without it, or GSL certification (e.g., Safety Coordinator) depends on it.

**Recommended (R):** The employee SHOULD complete these courses for professional development and career progression. The JD lists related competencies but the course is not a prerequisite for role performance.

**Decision criteria for M vs R:**
1. Is the competency listed as a qualification/requirement in the JD? -> M
2. Does a Management Directive explicitly require it? -> M
3. Is it required for a GSL certification (Safety Coordinator, Foreman Training Program)? -> M
4. Does the SCORM taxonomy list this role in `target_roles_draft`? -> M if Foundational difficulty, R if Intermediate/Advanced
5. Is it a general professional development competency (e.g., "Continuous Learning")? -> R
6. Is the competency listed in a higher-level role the employee is preparing for? -> R
