# GSL Academy - Category Structure

## Current State Assessment

The current training library has approximately **150+ SCORM modules** with significant redundancy (30+ duplicates) and no clear categorical hierarchy. Modules are mixed between full courses, chapter-level content, and standalone topics.

---

## Proposed Category Structure

### Level 1: Safety & Compliance

| Subcategory | Modules | Target Audience |
|-------------|---------|-----------------|
| **1.1 New Hire Orientation** | A Commitment to Zero Broken Lives, GSL New Hire Safety Orientation, Nevada's Employee Rights and Responsibilities | All new hires |
| **1.2 Electrical Safety (NFPA 70-E)** | 2024 NFPA 70-E Required Boundaries, Arc Flash PPE, Electrical Shock/Arc Flash Risk Assessments, Safe Electrical Work Practices 2024 NFPA 70-E | All electrical workers |
| **1.3 Lockout/Tagout (LOTO)** | LOTO Main Course, Simple LOTO, Complex LOTO, Abnormal Lockout Removal, Hazardous Energy Control, Return to Service, Verifying De-Energized Condition | All field personnel |
| **1.4 Fall Protection** | Fall Protection (main), Edges Openings and Guarding, Scaffolding Safety, Accessing a Scaffold, Working Safely on Scaffolding, Portable and Fixed Ladders | Workers at heights |
| **1.5 Confined Spaces** | Permit Required Confined Spaces | Confined space entrants |
| **1.6 Hazard Communication** | Hazard Communication, Container Labels, Safety Data Sheets, Written HazCom Plan, Types of Chemical Hazards | All field personnel |
| **1.7 General Safety** | Slips Trips and Falls, OSHA Focus Four, TRACK Hazard Control, Workplace Inspections | All personnel |
