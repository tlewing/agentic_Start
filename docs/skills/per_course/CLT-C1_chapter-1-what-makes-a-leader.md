### d. Instructional Content
- **Silent Leadership vs. Authoritarian Leadership**: Silent leaders are humble, listen, build trust, empower teams. Authoritarian leaders are ego-driven, demand obedience, impose plans.
- **Key Traits**: Adaptability, Balanced Direction, Emotional Intelligence.
- **Leadership at Every Level**: Leading across (peers), up (supervisors), and down (direct reports).
- **Overcoming Roadblocks**: Ownership — focus on what you can control. Steps: Assess, Build Relationships, Own Your Path.
- **Relationship Building**: Regular check-ins, seeking/providing mentorship, open communication.
- **Learning Objectives**: Define effective leadership, identify silent leader traits, understand empowering vs. authoritarian styles, learn relationship building, apply leadership skills.
