### d. Instructional Content
- **Extreme Ownership**: A true leader takes full responsibility. Example: 2006 friendly fire incident — Jocko took accountability, earned more trust.
- **Letting Go of Control**: Empower others instead of micromanaging. Set clear goals and trust teams to execute.
- **Leadership is for Everyone**: Leadership skills apply to all areas of life regardless of role.
- **What Makes a Great Leader**: Taking ownership, building trust, leading with humility, prioritizing team success.
