### b. Transcript Evidence (PRIMARY for this course)
VTT covers:
- **Ladder types**: Portable (straight, extendable, step) and fixed (permanently built into structures)
- **Inspection before use**: Check for defects, damage, rust, corrosion, broken/loose/missing parts. If problems found: take out of service, tag "Dangerous Do Not Use"
- **Setup — portable ladders**: Both feet firmly positioned and level. Use boards or ladder jack for uneven ground.
- **4:1 ratio**: For every 4 feet of vertical height, place bottom 1 foot out from the wall/structure
- **Extension above landing**: Ladder extends at least 3 feet above the climbing destination
- **Three-point contact**: Maintain two hands/one foot or two feet/one hand while climbing
