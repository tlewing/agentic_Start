### d. Instructional Content
- **Conditions for Simple LOTO**: Single hazardous energy source, qualified person review, no residual energy, complete de-energization, single lockout device, exclusive control, no hazards to others, no accident history.
- **Role of Qualified Person**: Foreman or designated safety coordinator physically reviews the circuit.
- **No Residual Energy**: No stored/residual energy that could reaccumulate after shutdown.
- **Single Lockout Device**: One device achieves and maintains locked-out condition.
- **End-of-Shift Protocols**: Remove personal lockout devices; apply isolation officer's lock if electrically safe condition cannot be established.
- **Exceptions**: Cord and plug-connected equipment if controlled by unplugging; plug under exclusive control.
