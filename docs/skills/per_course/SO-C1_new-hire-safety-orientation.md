### d. Instructional Content
- **L1: Commitment to Safety — A Shared Responsibility**: GSL's legacy, safety as a value (not just a priority), three-legged stool (safety/quality/productivity).
- **L2: A Commitment to Zero Broken Lives**: Concentric rings of protection, recognizing gaps, constant evaluation. Zero Broken Lives is more than a slogan.
- **L3: Employee Rights and Responsibilities**: State of Nevada SCATS. Slips/trips/falls causes.
- **L4: Hazard Awareness**: Job-site orientation, job-disaster plan, accident prevention handbook, medical services & first aid. Authority/obligation to stop unsafe work. 911 procedures.
- **L5: PPE**: Hardhats (company-issued only, sticker placement), safety glasses, gloves, hearing protection (85 dB), respiratory protection, reflective clothing.
- **L6: Fire Protection, Housekeeping, Vehicles**: Fire extinguisher awareness/inspection, housekeeping standards, company vehicle policies (MVR check, seat belts, pre-use inspection).
- **L7: Tools**: Power tool task training, PPE for tools, guards/safety equipment, cord management, GFCI requirements, defective tool procedures (red tag, send to shop). Powder actuated tools. Prohibited practices. Personal tools vs. company tools.
