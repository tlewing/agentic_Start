### d. Instructional Content
- **Self-Reflection Opening**: "When you make important leadership decisions, what guides your actions most?"
- **Learning Objectives**: Define VDL, recognize how values shape leadership, understand alignment between values and actions, apply VDL principles.
- **What Is Values-Driven Leadership**: Decisions grounded in core values. For Coach K: integrity, respect, trust, teamwork.
- **How Coach K Put Values into Action** (5 interactive items): Creating team culture of mutual respect; Demanding accountability in performance and character; Reinforcing "We Over Me" mindset; Leading with empathy in adversity; Developing great people, not just great players.
- **Lessons from Locker Room to Boardroom**: VDL applies beyond sports to business, classroom, community.
