### d. Instructional Content
- **L1: Communicate in Real Time** — Value of timely communication, when to communicate immediately, habits for clarity, building a culture of responsiveness.
- **L2: Create an Environment for Communication** — Psychological safety, respectful listening, consistency, confidentiality. Fostering open dialogue, modeling vulnerability.
- **L3: Elevate Your Meetings** — Purpose-driven meetings, timeboxing, encouraging participation, action-oriented follow-up. Common pitfalls and avoidance.
- **L4: Know When to be Quiet** — Silence as a leadership tool, when to stay quiet (after questions, emotional moments, when others need the floor), silence builds trust, self-awareness in communication.
