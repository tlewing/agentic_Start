SOP: 9.2.010 - Team Selection

Department: Project Management / Operations

Related SOPs:

·         9.2.015 - Project Turnover Meeting

·         9.2.040 - PM Reviews Plans, Specifications & Schedule

·         9.2.130 - Construction Execution Kickoff Meeting

 


PURPOSE

To establish a standardized process for selecting and assembling the project team immediately after project award, ensuring the right mix of skills, experience, and availability to meet project requirements.

 

 


SCOPE

Applies to all projects / situations where:

·         Project has been awarded

·         Project team needs to be assembled

·         Resource planning and assignment is required before kickoff

 

 


ROLES & RESPONSIBILITIES

Branch Manager (A)                

Selects Project Manager and assigns or approves final team assignments; Allocates resources across projects; Resolves resource conflicts.

Project Manager (R)                

Identifies required roles and skill sets; Recommends team members; Coordinates with Branch Manager on availability.

 

General Superintendent (C)             

Validates field leadership assignments; Confirms crew availability and capability

Safety Coordinator (C)                         

Ensures safety-qualified personnel are assigned, Reviews site-specific safety requirements.

Purchasing (I)                                              

Informed of procurement resource needs

Prefabrication Lead (I)                          

Informed of prefab support requirements

Project Coordinator (I)                         

Informed of administrative support assignments |

 

RACI: R=Responsible, A=Accountable, C=Consulted, I=Informed

 


REQUIREMENTS

·         Award package and project scope

·         Project schedule and duration

·         Resource availability matrix

·         Skills/certification requirements

·         Project complexity assessment

·         Historical performance data (similar projects)

 


PROCEDURE

STEP 1 - Assess Project Requirements

PM reviews award package and project scope to identify:

·         Required roles (PM, APM, Foreman, Field Supervisor, Safety, etc.)

·         Skill requirements (certifications, experience level)

·         Duration and timing of resource needs

·         Special requirements (BIM, prefab, commissioning)

 

STEP 2 - Review Resource Availability

PM coordinates with Branch Manager and GS to:

·         Review current project commitments

·         Identify available personnel

·         Consider geographical and logistical factors

·         Assess workload balance across projects

 

STEP 3 - Select Team Members

Based on requirements and availability:

·         Match skills to project needs

·         Consider team chemistry and past performance

·         Ensure appropriate supervision ratios

·         Address any certification gaps

 

STEP 4 - Obtain Approval

Branch Manager reviews and approves:

·         Final team roster

·         Resource allocation timing

·         Any cross-project resource sharing

·         Budget implications of team selection

 

STEP 5 - Communicate Assignments

PM communicates assignments to:

·         Selected team members

·         Supporting departments (Safety, Purchasing, Prefab)

·         Project Coordinator for administrative setup

·         Schedule turnover meeting (SOP 9.2.015)

 

 


COMPLIANCE & REVIEW

KPI

·         Team selection complete within 3 days of award - 100%

·         All required roles identified and filled - 100%

·         Branch Manager approval obtained - 100%

·         Team members notified before turnover meeting - 100%
