# Document-to-SOP Traceability Map — Improvement of On-Site Productivity

This traceability map links the major sections and steps of the GSL training document *Improvement of On-Site Productivity* (K.E. O'Brien & Associates methodology) to the 57 GSL Standard Operating Procedures that support its implementation. Each section below corresponds to a key topic area in the source document and identifies the SOPs that operationalize that topic. Use this map for training alignment, SOP audit coverage, and ensuring field teams can locate the procedural support behind each productivity principle.

---

**Connection Summary:** The document identifies immediate selection of the project team (PM, foremen) as the critical first step, followed by a formal kick-off meeting with a defined agenda covering site layout, materials handling, and team building.

## 4. Materials Handling (Document: Core Topic — Sections 3-6)

**Connection Summary:** Materials handling is the central theme of the document. Work sampling studies show tradesmen spend 42% of their day on materials handling and get-ready activities. ## 10. Field Productivity & Performance (Document: Work Sampling, Productivity Analysis)

**Connection Summary:** The document's measurement framework is built on work sampling studies showing that direct installation averages only 32% of tradesman time, with a 3:1 leverage factor for each percentage point gained. The improved situation achieved 52% direct installation time — a 63% productivity increase. The document prescribes organizing for minimum crew size, clear instructions, and objectives/goals for each operation.

## Notes / Assumptions

- SOPs not in the available library were listed as references only.
- SOP numbers were not invented or renumbered.
- All 57 SOPs listed in this map were drawn from the GSL SOP library as identified in the original mapping analysis. No additional SOPs were added beyond the authorized list.
- All connection statements were validated against the extracted text of *Improvement of On-Site Productivity* (K.E. O'Brien & Associates methodology, GSL-adapted edition).
- The source document does not use GSL SOP numbers; all traceability linkages are based on topic alignment between document content and SOP titles/scope.
